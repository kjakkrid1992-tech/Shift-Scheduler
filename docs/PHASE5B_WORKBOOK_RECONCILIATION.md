# Phase 5B — workbook reconciliation and implementation requirements
Status: source review completed; finalization policy pending owner verification. Phase 5A remains review-only.
Source: owner-provided local payroll workbook; reviewed its stored cell formulas and cached results. Do not commit workbook, employee names, bank account details, individual salary history or actual payment records.
## Verified structural findings
- 23 worksheets: three templates/reference sheets and twenty historical payroll sheets. Identified 208 payslip blocks, all with numeric income, deduction and net totals. Cached line sums and net reconcile for all 208 blocks. This is arithmetic reconciliation, not verification of legal entitlement or recalculation of historical attendance.
- Daily pay and hourly overtime are separately recorded. Standard cashier shift: nine elapsed hours including one hour break; normal eight hours. Combined shift: 06:30–21:00, two hours break, eight normal hours and 4.5 overtime hours.
- Reference worksheet specifies different tenure-based wage tiers for cashier and housekeeping. Final tenure boundary overlaps previous tier; owner must confirm effective date. Actual historical rates do not always match reference rate pairs. Effective-dated compensation_history remains authoritative; no automatic wage-rule enrollment.
- Historical sheets include fixed monthly salaries, daily wages, hourly and mixed payment examples, partial-day amounts, overtime, attendance bonus, performance/behavior bonuses, advances, withholding tax, social security, excess leave and lateness. Some entries are manually entered amounts rather than derivable formulas. Not every historical short/partial day can be inferred.
- Group sheets combine several trial/part-time staff in one document, sometimes recording the entire wage as an advance with zero remaining cash. Do not assume an actual advance occurred or assign group totals to one worker during import. Split by person with owner-reviewed attribution and payment evidence; support individual settlement documents and group payment summaries.
- Payslip fields include company name, pay period, payment date, employee name/position, payment destination, income and deduction lines, totals, net, annual accumulated values, payer/recipient signature and dates. Some sheets omit annual fields. Annual income formulas differ (net, income less social security, or gross in different periods). Do not import the displayed accumulated amount as taxable income without reconciliation.
## Implementation decisions proposed
1. Structured payroll lines with category, source, quantity/unit, effective rate, amount in satang, reason and evidence. Separate regular wages, OT, other earnings, tax, social security, advance allocation and other deductions. Keep manual exceptions explicit and audited; do not silently apply historical penalties.
2. For confirmed approved daily attendance, one full normal workday = one daily wage; combined shift = one day plus verified OT, not two daily wages. Hourly pay uses approved minutes and actual effective rate. Partial-day/leave/holiday/monthly proration, overtime classification, rounding and tenure transitions require policy confirmation before automatic final calculation.
3. Compute approved-source completeness and pay-run snapshot on server, use existing employment locks and add period/source locks; no finalization if missing inputs, pending attendance, unresolved rule, negative net or unreviewed deduction. Final payroll locks must also block retroactive wage changes, attendance reopen and advance void affecting the closed run; corrections require separately authorized adjustment run.
4. Advance settlement uses actual paid records with explicit allocation, outstanding balance, lawful deduction eligibility, consent/evidence and no double deduction. Never interpret a historical zero-net payslip as proof of a paid advance. Do not assume every paid advance can be deducted in full.
5. Owner approval, separate actual disbursement recording, immutable approved snapshot and printable official individual payslip. No direct payment transfer. Keep payroll confidentiality and restrict sensitive payment data; never expose bank information via public frontend or repository.
6. Historical data migration: staging, detect repeated blocks, verify period/date mismatches, map individuals to employment records, preserve original source references, flag aggregate sheets and missing identities, reconcile before import. Do not overwrite old payroll records.
## Required policy checks before production
- exact tenure effective date; partial day and mixed pay; monthly proration and treatment of approved leave, holidays and excess absence; normal and overtime minute rounding and exceptions
- social security scope/base/caps, withholding tax and annual reporting definitions; lawful wage/OT and deduction rules must be independently verified for the applicable date, not inferred from old workbook
- advance deduction consent/cap/allocation, trial/part-time immediate payment classification, handling of residual balances and grouped payment summaries
- payment date vs period, annual totals definition, bank data access and historical corrections
## Synthetic regression examples
- Daily rate 400 THB, 20 full normal workdays including 2 combined shifts, OT rate 80 THB/h, 9 approved OT hours: base 8,000, OT 720; candidate advance deduction 1,000 after review: net 7,720 before other items.
- Rate changes within period: 10 eligible full workdays at 400 and 10 at 420 = 8,200; use per-day rate, not latest rate multiplied by all days.
- Half-day without approved company policy: block automatic finalization rather than guess a fraction.
- Missing attendance, missing rate, paid advance already allocated, voided advance, closed payroll source change, concurrent approval, zero-net group record, YTD definition and rehire across periods: explicit regression cases.
